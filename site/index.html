Who it is for

The average commuter in Montréal, especially in high-density areas where many types of transport come together.

Why it matters

Right now the average commuter cannot easily find out whether it is more convenient to take a Bixi or the metro for a short distance, especially when there are delays in service or bikes are running out.

Looking at the metro together with Bixi availability gives people faster access to real-time data, in one place, so they can pick the quickest option themselves.

What you can do here

Choose take a bike or return a bike (top right). Every Bixi station is coloured by how many bikes or free docks it has right now. Click a metro station (the coloured M) to see the Bixi bikes and docks within a 4-minute walk (300 m).

What this is not

This is not another routing app. It does not plan your trip; it shows live availability and STM service notices for short distances so you can decide.
